5. **Follow-up threading:** standalone `Re:` drafts exist. They are not genuine Gmail reply drafts because the originals have not been sent. They must remain marked as prepared only.
6. **Compliance:** four selected recipients are ineligible until subscriber status is resolved or consent/exception is documented.
