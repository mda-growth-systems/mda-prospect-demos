Creative directions — A2M TEC Ltd
Date: 2026-08-05

Direction 1 — Dark control-room console (selected)
Visual style: Night visual system built around the operational language of commercial security, gates, barriers, cctv and access control.
Layout approach: A bespoke console composition with the interactive tool visible early.
Primary customer journey: Multi-site security survey and existing-platform qualification.
Signature interactive feature: Security scope console.
Why it fits: It turns the business's most valuable first conversation into a concise, usable brief.
Difference: The layout, typography, interaction and section order are unique to this prospect.

Direction 2 — Field report journal
Visual style: Documentary project notes, annotated capabilities and proof-led service panels.
Layout approach: Vertical project journal with anchored service navigation.
Primary customer journey: Start from a completed-project type, then request a comparable survey or service.
Signature interactive feature: Expandable project-scope cards.
Why it fits: Strong for prospects that want to lead with technical experience.
Difference: More editorial and proof-led than the selected operational intake approach.

Direction 3 — Service-area command centre
Visual style: Geographic coverage map, response bands and service-area cards.
Layout approach: Map-first hero followed by regional service routes.
Primary customer journey: Confirm service area, then route to the relevant team.
Signature interactive feature: Postcode and service-area checker prototype.
Why it fits: Useful for nationwide or multi-region coverage.
Difference: Geography-led rather than the selected workflow-led design.

Selection rationale
Direction 1 was selected because it demonstrates a specific business outcome without criticising the existing site: multi-site security survey and existing-platform qualification.
