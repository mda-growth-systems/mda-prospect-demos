Falcon Security Systems — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Security command matrix

DIRECTION 1: Security command matrix
Visual style: Dark green command-board with privacy-conscious site tiles
Layout / customer journey: Commercial buyers scope systems without exposing sensitive details
Signature feature: Security survey matrix
Why it fits: The business spans four connected security disciplines
Difference from the other demos: Uses a camera-grid visual and controlled disclosure

DIRECTION 2: Canterbury service desk
Visual style: Local, trustworthy editorial design
Layout / customer journey: Local customers choose survey, service or emergency support
Signature feature: Service-desk switchboard
Why it fits: Fits a long-established family business
Difference from the other demos: Prioritises approachable local service

DIRECTION 3: Integrated protection layers
Visual style: Layered building illustration
Layout / customer journey: Buyers see how alarm, CCTV and access control connect
Signature feature: Protection-layer toggles
Why it fits: Explains integrated systems clearly
Difference from the other demos: Uses a building cutaway as the core interaction
