Fire Alert UK — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Impairment response

DIRECTION 1: Impairment response
Visual style: Bold red-and-charcoal emergency hierarchy
Layout / customer journey: Users separate active impairment from planned work immediately
Signature feature: Fire-system priority route
Why it fits: Life-safety enquiries need unambiguous priority
Difference from the other demos: Uses a response band and safety-state cards

DIRECTION 2: South West coverage
Visual style: Regional map and local service tiles
Layout / customer journey: Customers confirm location and service availability
Signature feature: Coverage-to-service router
Why it fits: Makes the territory clear
Difference from the other demos: Map-led structure is unique in the batch

DIRECTION 3: Service-record journey
Visual style: Clean maintenance ledger
Layout / customer journey: Existing clients book recurring checks and new clients request surveys
Signature feature: Next-service planner
Why it fits: Supports maintenance renewals
Difference from the other demos: Designed around records and due dates
