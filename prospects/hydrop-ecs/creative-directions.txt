DIRECTION 1: Compliance dossier
Visual style: Calm editorial green with document tabs and evidence hierarchy
Layout / customer journey: Duty holders define estate, responsibility and required assurance
Signature feature: Compliance scope planner
Why it fits: The service is based on governance and evidence
Difference from the other demos: Looks like a professional compliance dossier

DIRECTION 2: Water asset map
Visual style: Illustrated building-water network
Layout / customer journey: Facilities teams identify assets and task coverage
Signature feature: Asset-to-task explorer
Why it fits: Connects naturally to COMPASS and PPM
Difference from the other demos: Uses a network visual instead of service cards

DIRECTION 3: Training and assurance hub
Visual style: Calendar-led interface with programme pathways
Layout / customer journey: Users choose training, consultancy or platform support
Signature feature: Role-based learning selector
Why it fits: Useful for responsible-person development
Difference from the other demos: Centres the journey on roles and competence
