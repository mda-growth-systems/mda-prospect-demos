iPro Hire — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Depot-first catalogue

DIRECTION 1: Depot-first catalogue
Visual style: Industrial yellow, black and off-white with category-led navigation
Layout / customer journey: A customer starts with the task and sees matching hire categories
Signature feature: Interactive job-to-equipment brief
Why it fits: It mirrors how customers think before they know an exact machine name
Difference from the other demos: Uses a catalogue rail and depot switcher rather than a standard agency hero

DIRECTION 2: Project planner
Visual style: Clean blueprint grid with date-led planning
Layout / customer journey: A contractor builds a temporary site setup
Signature feature: Multi-item project checklist
Why it fits: Useful for commercial projects needing several equipment categories
Difference from the other demos: Focuses on planning rather than browsing

DIRECTION 3: Rapid quote card
Visual style: Compact mobile-first cards with strong phone access
Layout / customer journey: A customer sends the minimum quote details in under a minute
Signature feature: Progressive four-step quote card
Why it fits: Fits urgent local hire behaviour
Difference from the other demos: Designed as a fast mobile utility
