MPH Elec Services LTD — Netlify-ready concept demo

FILES
- index.html: main demo page
- thank-you.html: form success destination
- styles.css: responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation and current-year helper
- verification-notes.txt: research and factual limits
- outreach-email.txt: first-contact draft (not sent)
- follow-up-email.txt: follow-up draft (not scheduled or sent)

DEPLOYMENT
1. Upload this individual folder to Netlify as its own site, or drag the ZIP into Netlify Drop.
2. Confirm index.html is at the deployment root.
3. In Netlify, check Forms after the first deploy. The form name is: mph-elec-services-enquiry
4. Submit a test enquiry and confirm the redirect to thank-you.html.
5. Replace the placeholder demo URL in both email text files before outreach.

FORM
The form uses method=POST, data-netlify=true, a hidden form-name field and a honeypot. It includes industry-specific lead-qualification fields and redirects to thank-you.html.
