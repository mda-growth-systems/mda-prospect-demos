Nova Renewables — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Feasibility landscape

DIRECTION 1: Feasibility landscape
Visual style: Warm solar gradient with roof-and-load visualisation
Layout / customer journey: Businesses test whether a site is ready for a feasibility conversation
Signature feature: Solar feasibility snapshot
Why it fits: Commercial solar depends on site and load data
Difference from the other demos: Uses a sun-path and load-profile illustration

DIRECTION 2: Project pathway
Visual style: Clean green step sequence
Layout / customer journey: Buyers see feasibility, design, DNO, installation and commissioning
Signature feature: DNO readiness checklist
Why it fits: Explains a complex buying process
Difference from the other demos: Centres on approvals and project stages

DIRECTION 3: Energy opportunity board
Visual style: Data-led dashboard
Layout / customer journey: Facilities teams compare solar, battery and EV charging priorities
Signature feature: Energy-stack selector
Why it fits: Supports cross-sell without overwhelming the user
Difference from the other demos: Looks like an energy planning tool
