Seward Refrigeration — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Cold-chain control room

DIRECTION 1: Cold-chain control room
Visual style: Deep blue control-room panels with icy data accents
Layout / customer journey: Operations teams classify risk before requesting support
Signature feature: Temperature-and-production risk snapshot
Why it fits: Industrial refrigeration is operationally critical
Difference from the other demos: Uses gauges and alarm panels rather than generic service cards

DIRECTION 2: Engineering schematic
Visual style: White technical drawings and pipe routes
Layout / customer journey: Project teams scope new systems and upgrades
Signature feature: System-design requirement map
Why it fits: Fits design and commissioning projects
Difference from the other demos: Centres on system architecture

DIRECTION 3: Three-region service map
Visual style: Regional layout linking Manchester, Yorkshire and East Anglia
Layout / customer journey: Customers select the nearest service region
Signature feature: Branch-aware incident router
Why it fits: Reflects the real three-office structure
Difference from the other demos: Map-led hero differentiates it from other demos
