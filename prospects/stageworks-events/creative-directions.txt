CREATIVE DIRECTIONS — Stageworks Events

1. Poster direction (selected)
Visual style: poster visual system with #f23fd0 as the signal colour.
Layout: business-specific hero, verified services and a dedicated event brief builder.
Primary journey: qualify production brief builder with venue, audience, scope and power qualification.
Signature interaction: Event brief builder.
Why selected: it turns the clearest operational friction into the centre of the concept.
Distinctive point: custom composition, industry fields and original inline illustration.

2. Service-library direction
Visual style: searchable capability index.
Layout: service taxonomy before enquiry.
Primary journey: explore services, then request advice.
Signature interaction: service filter.
Why not selected: less focused on the highest-value first-contact problem.

3. Case-led direction
Visual style: editorial project stories and process diagrams.
Layout: outcomes first, services second.
Primary journey: identify a similar project and request a survey.
Signature interaction: project-type selector.
Why not selected: verified project assets were not sufficient for a strong factual case-study concept.
