UK Lift & Escalator Company — Creative Directions
Selected direction: Asset status console

DIRECTION 1: Asset status console
Visual style: Dark technical dashboard with cyan status indicators
Layout / customer journey: A caller reports asset and risk before describing symptoms
Signature feature: Downtime priority console
Why it fits: It reflects a 24/7 engineering operation
Difference from the other demos: Uses live-status panels and a vertical shaft visual

DIRECTION 2: Vertical journey
Visual style: Bright corporate layout moving floor by floor
Layout / customer journey: A buyer moves through maintenance, repair, modernisation and installation
Signature feature: Interactive floor selector
Why it fits: Makes the service range easy to understand
Difference from the other demos: Navigation behaves like lift controls

DIRECTION 3: Reliability ledger
Visual style: Editorial white layout with maintenance records
Layout / customer journey: Facilities managers compare contract coverage
Signature feature: Maintenance-plan comparator
Why it fits: Supports recurring service decisions
Difference from the other demos: Centres on asset records rather than emergency response
